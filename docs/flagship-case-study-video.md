# Fulcrum Flagship Case Study Film

## Purpose

Show the emotional and operational shift from a successful founder acting as
the company's integration layer to a controlled, human-first AI operating
capability.

The story must not imply that Fulcrum connects an entire company to one
uncontrolled agent. Fulcrum maps the whole business, proves one high-value
workflow, keeps human approval where risk lives, and expands from measured
results.

## Runway Prompt

```text
Cinematic realistic brand film about a successful founder of a modern
mid-sized commerce company. He begins confidently in a bright
warehouse-office, then sees competitors responding at lightning speed as
abstract market signals race across several monitors. He feels outpaced but
determined. A calm expert operations team maps his business, and a refined
luminous network connects catalog, customer service, analytics, shipping,
warehouse and finance through visible human approval checkpoints. End with
the founder calmly leading his team and speaking with a customer while the
connected operation moves quickly in the background. Human-first,
trustworthy, warm amber and deep navy palette, documentary realism, elegant
camera movement. No readable text, logos, humanoid robots, science-fiction
holograms, layoffs or dystopia.
```

## Narrative Beats

1. Success created complexity.
2. Competitive speed exposed the founder bottleneck.
3. Fulcrum mapped the operation and connected one controlled workflow.
4. The business moved faster while people returned attention to customers and
   leadership.

## Accessibility Description

A successful commerce founder watches his growing operation, notices
competitors moving faster, works with an expert team to connect business
systems through controlled approval points, and returns his attention to
employees and customers while the operation moves reliably in the background.
