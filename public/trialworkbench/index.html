Why it matters

Most AI chats create reassurance. Trial Workbench creates reviewable structure.

The app keeps source-backed evidence, timeline rows, actor records, claim review, research drafts, review flags, and exports inside one workflow instead of scattered conversations.

Semantic case memory

Hybrid evidence retrieval searches by meaning and keyword across citation-safe case evidence.

Case spine

Master case context, timeline, actor map, evidence graph, claims, incidents, and strategy stay connected.

Review flags

Known weaknesses and proof gaps are surfaced for review instead of hidden behind confident language.

Current workflow

Grounded in the modules that exist today.

The live workflow is Intake → Evidence → Research → Claims Review → Strategy → Attorney Handoff, with advanced review for Timeline, Actor Map, Contract Strategy, and Precedent.

Intake

Athena intake, opening workflow, gap audit, contract understanding, actor/date scope, and candidate evidence queue.

Evidence

Uploads, Gmail sync/reindex hooks, evidence graph search, proposals, and contract routing.

Research

Research sessions, diagnostics, theory suggestions, memo drafts, CourtListener / RECAP metadata, and precedent items where records are present.

Handoff

Best evidence, prima facie, attorney handoff, export downloads, case refresh, and staged output approval.

Attorney handoff package

What the current package actually contains.

The handoff package consolidates active stored outputs: master case context, timeline highlights, actor summary, case strategy, incidents, attack table, best evidence, precedent, prima facie summary, review flags, and exportable JSON.

Matter summary

Title, dispute type, summary, harm statement, and relief items from master case context.

Timeline highlights

Verified timeline rows with labels and source types.

Actor summary

Actor names, roles, sides, and confidence scores.

Attack table

Attack points, priorities, and confidence scores.

Precedent summary

Stored precedent labels and linked precedent items where available.

Review flags

Known weaknesses and proof gaps for attorney or operator review.
